Custom Putting Greens for Windsor and Essex County Homes

A backyard putting green only works if it rolls true year after year. Through clay-soil settlement. Through Windsor's freeze-thaw cycles. Through the south-facing summer sun most Essex County yards get.

That means a different spec than a standard backyard install — a tighter aggregate base, more careful contouring during the build, and a low-profile turf rated to a specific stimp range.

We design every green around the layout you want. Single-hole practice, multi-hole short course, or pitching-and-putting combo. The contours get tuned during the base build so the ball behaves the way it should from day one.

How It Works

Turf Installed in 4 Simple Steps

From first call to finished yard — here's what the process looks like with Windsor Turf Pros.

1

Layout Design & Quote

We visit your Windsor property to discuss your layout — hole count, shape, fringe areas, and any slopes. You'll leave the consultation with a clear written quote.

2

Excavation & Base Build

We excavate to the correct depth, shape contours if desired, and build a compacted aggregate base that ensures proper drainage and a stable roll surface.

3

Turf Installation & Cup Setting

Performance putting green turf is cut and fitted precisely. Cups, pins, and any fringe sections are installed and secured during this phase.

4

We test the roll across every hole, inspect transitions between zones, and walk you through the finished green before we leave.

Common Questions

Putting Greens — Frequently Asked Questions

How realistic is the putting experience on an artificial putting green?
Using stimp-rated turf and a proper base, our greens deliver consistent, realistic roll. Most golfers find the feel comparable to a club green after a short adjustment period.
Can I add a chipping fringe around my putting green in Windsor?
Absolutely. We commonly install multi-zone setups with a chipping fringe and rough perimeter — just bring your layout ideas to the free estimate.
What maintenance does a backyard putting green need?
Very little — occasional brushing to keep fibres upright and a rinse after heavy debris accumulation. There's no irrigation, no mowing, and no seasonal shutdown.
How long does a custom putting green take to install in Windsor?
Most residential greens — single-hole through three-hole layouts — install in one to two days. The base build takes the longest because contouring has to be exact for the roll to behave correctly; turf cutting and cup-setting move quickly once the base passes a level check.
Do I need to water, fertilize, or seed a putting green to keep it playing right?
No watering, no seeding, no aeration. Maintenance is brushing the fibres upright every few months, topping up silica sand once a year, and rinsing debris off as needed. There's no irrigation cost and no seasonal shutdown — the green plays the same way in April and October.
